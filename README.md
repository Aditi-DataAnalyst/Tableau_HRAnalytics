 Tableau_HRAnalytics
Tableau dashboard analyzing employee attrition and retention trends with KPIs and workforce insights across departments,age groups,and job roles.

Features
- KPIs: Total Employees, Attrition Rate, Average Years at Company
- Department‑wise attrition analysis
- Age group attrition distribution
- Job role attrition comparison
- Employee count by department with attrition overlay
